Levelix Pro

How do I unlock Pro?

Choose a plan under Pricing on the Home page and pay. We then email a Pro code to the address you paid with. Open Levelix, sign in with the account you want Pro on, open the account menu (top right) and choose I have a code — or tap Already paid? under Pricing — and paste the code.

I paid, but no code has arrived

Codes are usually sent within a few hours, and at the latest within one working day. Check your spam folder first; if it is not there, write to us from the email address you paid with.

My code says it has been used or has run out

A code works once, for the account that redeems it, and only for the period it was issued for. Write to us with the code and we will sort it out.

Does Pro renew by itself?

No. Pro is a one-off payment for one month or one year. When it ends, your account goes back to Free — nothing is deleted — and you can buy Pro again whenever you like.

Can I get my money back?

Yes. Within 14 days of paying you get a full refund, for any reason: email us from the address you paid with. See the terms for the details.

Your tasks and your account

Do my tasks sync between devices?

Only when you are signed in. Without an account, your tasks stay in the browser you use — handy for trying Levelix, but clearing that browser's data deletes them.

What happens at 100 tasks on Free?

Free holds up to 100 open tasks. Finished tasks do not count, so ticking off or deleting tasks makes room again. Pro removes the limit.

How do I delete my data?

Signed in, open the account menu and choose Clear account to delete every task, on every device. To delete the account itself, write to us and we will remove it. More in the privacy policy.
