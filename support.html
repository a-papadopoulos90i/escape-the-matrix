Levelix Pro

How do I unlock Pro?

The simplest way: sign in to Levelix first, then choose a plan under Pricing on the Home page and pay. Pro turns on for that account by itself a few seconds after the payment goes through.

Paid without signing in? We email a Pro code to the address you paid with. Open Levelix, sign in with the account you want Pro on, open the account menu (top right) and choose I have a code — or tap Already paid? under Pricing — and paste the code.

I paid, but no code has arrived

Codes are sent automatically, normally within minutes. Check your spam folder first; if it is not there, write to us from the email address you paid with and we will sort it out within one working day.

My code says it has been used or has run out

A code works once, for the account that redeems it, and only for the period it was issued for. Write to us with the code and we will sort it out.

Does Pro renew by itself?

No. Pro is a one-off payment for one month or one year. When it ends, your account goes back to Free — nothing is deleted — and you can buy Pro again whenever you like.

Can I get my money back?

Yes. Within 14 days of paying you get a full refund, for any reason. Signed in, open the account menu, press the gear next to Sign out and choose Refund: the money goes back to your card at once (your bank may take a few days to show it) and Pro ends. You can also email us from the address you paid with. See the terms for the details.

Your tasks and your account

Do my tasks sync between devices?

Only when you are signed in. Without an account, your tasks stay in the browser you use — handy for trying Levelix, but clearing that browser's data deletes them.

What happens at 100 tasks on Free?

Free holds up to 100 open tasks. Finished tasks do not count, so ticking off or deleting tasks makes room again. Pro removes the limit.

Can I sign in with both Google and Apple?

Yes. Sign in with one, open the account menu, press the gear next to Sign out and choose Connect Apple (or Connect Google). From then on either button opens the same account. If that Apple or Google login already made a Levelix account of its own, delete that one first.

I wrote tasks before signing in — where are they?

They wait in your browser. Once you sign in, the dot on your picture turns red: open the account menu and press Sync to move them into your account.

How do I delete my data?

Signed in, open the account menu and press the gear next to Sign out. Clear account deletes every task, on every device. Delete account deletes the account itself, with its tasks, settings and Pro time — for good. More in the privacy policy.
